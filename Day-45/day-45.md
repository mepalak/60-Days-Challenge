# 🧠 AI Decision Strategist – Think Better. Decide Smarter.

An AI-powered decision support system that helps users evaluate options, compare alternatives, assess risks, and make well-informed decisions using structured reasoning.

> **Day 45 Project** of the **#60DayClaudeChallenge**

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

Making important decisions can be overwhelming when multiple factors are involved.

**AI Decision Strategist** acts as an intelligent thinking partner by:

- Identifying available options
- Listing pros and cons
- Performing weighted comparisons
- Estimating risks
- Suggesting the most suitable choice
- Explaining the reasoning behind every recommendation

Whether you're choosing a career, buying a laptop, planning investments, or selecting a project idea, this AI assistant provides structured guidance.
