## 🚀 Project Overview

**Verdict Engine** is an AI-powered decision-making assistant designed to help users evaluate difficult choices.

Instead of simply saying *"It depends,"* Verdict Engine analyzes a decision from multiple perspectives, identifies important trade-offs, evaluates risks and benefits, and delivers a clear **verdict**.

The goal is not to make decisions blindly, but to help users **think more clearly, objectively, and strategically**.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Many decisions are difficult because they involve:

* Multiple options
* Conflicting priorities
* Unclear risks
* Emotional bias
* Short-term vs. long-term consequences
* Incomplete information



---

## 💡 Solution

The Verdict Engine takes a user's decision and evaluates it using multiple dimensions.

### Decision Analysis Framework

1. **Understand the Decision**
2. **Identify Available Options**
3. **Analyze Pros & Cons**
4. **Evaluate Risks**
5. **Consider Long-Term Impact**
6. **Compare Alternatives**
7. **Calculate a Decision Score**
8. **Generate the Final Verdict**
9. **Explain the Reasoning**
10. **Suggest the Next Best Action**
