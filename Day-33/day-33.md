🚀 Day 33 – Think Like a Marketing Strategist: Grow This Brand
📌 Overview

Day 33 of the 60 Days Claude AI Challenge focused on building an interactive learning application that teaches users how marketers think rather than simply generating marketing content. The project simulates the decision-making process of professional marketing strategists, helping beginners understand branding, positioning, customer segmentation, and campaign planning through an engaging web application.

🎯 Objective

Develop a single-page HTML application that enables users to:

Understand strategic marketing thinking.
Analyze a business from a marketer's perspective.
Build customer personas.
Identify unique value propositions.
Create marketing strategies.
Learn branding fundamentals through interactive exercises.
