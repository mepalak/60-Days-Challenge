# 🤖 Day 43 – Build AI Workflow Architect
### Design Complete AI-Powered Workflows

> Create, visualize, and automate intelligent workflows using AI to streamline business processes, decision-making, and task execution.

---

## 📌 Overview

AI Workflow Architect is an intelligent workflow design system that helps users create end-to-end AI-powered workflows for different use cases such as customer support, content creation, HR automation, sales pipelines, education, and software development.

The system maps every step, recommends AI tools, automates repetitive tasks, and generates optimized workflows with minimal manual effort.
