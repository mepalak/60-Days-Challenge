# 🚀 Day 34 – Claude AI Challenge

# 🕵️ Marketing Detective: Campaign Investigation Simulator

> **An AI-powered interactive marketing investigation simulator where users analyze campaign data, uncover hidden insights, identify marketing problems, and solve business cases using evidence-based decision making.**

---

## 📖 Overview

Welcome to **Day 34** of my **60 Days Claude AI Challenge**.

Today's project focused on learning **how successful marketers think like investigators**. Rather than relying on intuition, great marketers collect evidence, analyze customer behavior, identify patterns, and continuously optimize campaigns based on data.

Using **Claude AI**, I built an engaging detective-style educational application where users investigate marketing campaigns, examine business metrics, discover customer insights, and solve real-world marketing challenges.

This project transforms marketing education into an interactive problem-solving experience.

---

# 🎯 Challenge Objective

Build an educational web application that teaches users to:

- Investigate marketing campaigns
- Analyze business metrics
- Discover customer behavior patterns
- Identify campaign strengths and weaknesses
- Make data-driven marketing decisions
- Improve campaign performance using evidence

Instead of guessing what works, users learn **how professional marketers validate every decision with data.**

---

# 💡 Problem Statement

Many beginners believe marketing success comes from creativity alone.

In reality, modern marketing depends on:

- Customer data
- Campaign analytics
- Audience behavior
- Conversion tracking
- Performance optimization
- Continuous experimentation

This simulator helps users understand how evidence drives every successful marketing strategy.
