WHAT IS THIS Personal AI Playbook

This is a personal toolkit for turning how you already use AI into reusable, editable systems — instead of re-typing a new prompt from scratch every time you debug, review code, write tests, or draft a PR description. Everything is saved in your browser (local storage), nothing leaves this file.

  • Dashboard & CategoriesBrowse ready-made workflows grouped by the kind of task they help with.
  • Prompt BuilderAssemble a custom prompt piece by piece (role, objective, constraints, etc.) with a live preview.
  • Loop BuilderTurn any prompt into a self-checking, iterative "run until done" prompt.
  • Your own workflowsCreate, edit, duplicate, favorite, and search workflows — saved automatically.

HOW IT WORKS Loop Builder

A looping prompt is one that tells the AI to keep re-checking and improving its own answer against your criteria, instead of stopping after one pass — useful for things like "keep refactoring until all tests would pass" or "keep investigating until you find the root cause." Paste in a normal prompt (or one of your saved workflow templates), define what "done" looks like and what it isn't allowed to do, and this turns it into a self-checking loop prompt.

What "success" means in one sentence — the loop keeps working toward this specific target.
How the AI should judge its own attempt before deciding whether to try again.
What the AI should actually change between attempts if the first one falls short.
Exactly when the loop should end — success, a retry limit, or a dead end. Prevents endless spinning.
