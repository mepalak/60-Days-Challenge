🧩 Day 35 – Build Prompt Puzzle: Master AI Prompting Through Play
📌 Overview

Welcome to Day 35 of the 60 Days of Claude AI Challenge!

Today's project focuses on learning one of the most valuable AI skills—Prompt Engineering—through an interactive puzzle game.

Instead of reading prompt-writing rules, users solve engaging challenges by arranging prompt components into the correct order. This gamified experience helps beginners understand how effective prompts are structured while making learning enjoyable.

🎯 Objective

Build a single-page interactive HTML application called:

Prompt Puzzle: Master AI Prompting Through Play

The application should teach users how to write better prompts by solving progressively challenging puzzles.
